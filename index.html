How it works

Three quiet steps.

No onboarding quiz, no productivity system to learn. Start a timer, do the work, and let the garden keep the record.

  1. 01

    Choose a session

    Pick focus, a short break, or a long break. Add a task label if it helps. Start when you are ready — the timer keeps running even if the screen sleeps.

  2. 02

    Let the garden grow

    Finish the session and a bloom appears. Break sessions and interrupted timers never take one away, so a difficult day cannot undo your garden.

  3. 03

    Look back gently

    See the week, the heatmap, and the patterns in your history. Use it to understand your time, not to grade it.

The timer works fully offline and your focus data is never uploaded. A tester code optionally uses Firebase; the core app needs no account.

Privacy

Private by design, not by promise.

Sproutime has no account for the core timer, no analytics, and no cloud sync. Your timer, history, garden, notes, tags, and routines live in the app's private storage on your phone. Google Play Billing handles purchases. If you choose to redeem a tester code, an optional Firebase anonymous account stores the redemption and its 30-day expiry. The core app works without it.
