Getting started

The basics of the app and what it needs from you.

Is Sproutime free?

Yes. The core of Sproutime is free forever: focus and break timers, history and day logs, stats, CSV export and import, every theme, accessibility settings, and your garden.

Plus is an optional subscription that adds guided and custom routines, soundscapes, weekly challenges, Year in Focus, widget tints and content controls, and garden layouts and seasons. Grovekeeper is a one-time purchase that includes every Plus feature permanently.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed for the timer. There is no sign-up, login, email, or profile for normal use. The app works offline, including the timer, history, garden, routines, and bundled soundscapes.

Google Play Billing needs a connection when you open the Plans screen, buy a plan, or restore a purchase. If you choose to redeem a 30-day tester code, Sproutime creates an optional Firebase anonymous account; that is the only time identity data is involved, and it is still optional.

Which Android versions are supported?

Sproutime supports Android 8.0 (API 26) and later. It is designed and tested for recent Android versions and uses Material 3 styling with the app's own warm paper palette.

What is the difference between focus, short break, and long break?

Focus is the work interval. Short break is the pause between focus sessions. Long break comes after your chosen number of focus sessions in a cycle. All three lengths, and the number of sessions per cycle, can be changed in Settings in one-minute steps.

Only completed focus sessions grow a bloom. Breaks are for rest and never add or remove plants.

Timers & routines

What happens while a session is running, and after.

Does the timer keep running if I close the app or lock my phone?

Yes. The running session is saved locally, so closing the app or locking the screen does not stop it. Reopening Sproutime restores the countdown, and the home-screen widget can show and control it.

A completion is recorded once per session, even if the app and the notification both try to finalize it at the same moment.

What happens to my timer if my phone restarts?

Sproutime saves the session and restores it after a restart. Within a single boot it uses Android's monotonic clock so the countdown cannot be thrown off by a time-zone or clock change; after a reboot it falls back to the wall clock.

Alarm and widget updates are rescheduled after boot. If the session finished while the phone was off, the app reconciles the saved state the next time it runs.

Why did my completion notification not appear?

Check three things:

  • Android's notification permission for Sproutime. Settings inside the app shows whether notifications are allowed or blocked, with a shortcut to Android's notification settings.
  • Exact-alarm access. If Android does not allow exact alarms, Sproutime falls back to an inexact alarm, which can arrive a little late.
  • Battery restrictions. Some phone makers pause background work aggressively. Allow Sproutime to run in the background if alerts are consistently late.

The timer itself still completes and records the session even when the alert is delayed or blocked.

What are routines, and how many steps can I add?

Routines run a sequence of focus and break blocks automatically and carry your task through the sequence. The four built-in routines are Classic, Deep Work, Study, and Short Reset.

Plus adds custom routines. You can name a sequence and add focus and break steps in one-minute increments, up to 24 steps per routine. Custom routines are saved on your device.

Can the next session start automatically?

Yes. Turn on Auto-start next session in Settings and the next break, then the next focus, begins without pressing Continue. It is off by default, so a session never starts without you unless you asked it to.

Garden & Collection

How focus sessions become flowers.

How does a session become a flower?

Complete a focus session and one bloom is added to the current patch. Each patch holds twelve blooms; when it is full, the next bloom starts a new patch. Older patches are kept and can be reached by swiping.

Breaks and interrupted sessions never take a flower away. Deleting your history is the only action that clears the garden.

Why did my garden reset after I deleted history?

The garden, stats, and Collection are derived from your session history. Settings → Delete history removes the sessions and everything built from them, including the garden and Collection discoveries. The confirmation dialog says this before you proceed, and it cannot be undone.

Export a CSV first if you want a copy of the underlying sessions. Settings, routines, and your plan are not affected by deleting history.

How many flower species are there?

Twelve species across five rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Mythic. The Moonbell is the only Mythic bloom and appears in roughly 0.2% of sessions. The Collection shows what you have discovered without revealing the ones you have not.

Do garden layouts or seasons move my flowers?

No. Layouts and seasonal palettes change only how the garden is drawn. Sessions, patches, and flower positions are tied to your history and never move when you change a layout or season.

Seasonal palettes and garden layouts are part of Plus. The standard garden remains available on the free plan.

Why is the same flower appearing again?

Species are chosen from a weighted pool, so common flowers appear more often than rare ones. Duplicates are normal and still count as a bloom in your garden — the Collection tracks which species you have seen, not how many of each you own.

Data, backups & privacy

Everything stays on this device unless you export it.

Where is my data stored?

In a private DataStore file called focus_grove inside the app's sandbox. It holds settings, timer state, session history, custom routines, challenge state, and plan status. Other apps cannot read it on a normal, non-rooted device.

Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Sproutime, so the file is not copied to Google Drive or automatically moved to a new phone. Use CSV export if you want a portable copy.

How do I export or import my history?

Go to Settings → Export history. Sproutime creates a CSV and opens Android's document picker so you choose where to save it. To restore, use Settings → Import history and pick a Sproutime CSV.

Import merges sessions from another phone, skips duplicate rows, and rebuilds the garden, stats, and Collection from the merged history. Exported files are outside the app's protected storage, so store them somewhere you trust.

Does uninstalling the app delete my data?

Yes. Uninstalling Sproutime removes its private storage, including history, settings, and routines. Android may keep some data if you only clear the cache; to remove everything without uninstalling, use Clear storage in Android Settings.

A purchase is not deleted. Plus and Grovekeeper can be restored from Google Play with the same Google account after reinstalling.

What data does Sproutime collect?

None of your timer, history, garden, tag, or note content leaves your device. There is no account for the core app, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no cloud sync. Google Play Billing handles optional purchases. If you choose to redeem a tester code, an optional Firebase anonymous account stores the redemption, hashed code, and 30-day expiry.

The full detail, including permissions and retention, is in the privacy policy.

Why does a privacy-first app need the internet permission?

Google Play Billing needs it to contact Google Play when you view plans, buy something, or restore a purchase. The permission is not used to upload your sessions, send analytics, or load remote content. Soundscapes are bundled in the app and never streamed.

Widgets & notifications

Glanceable controls and local alerts.

Which widgets are included?

There are five home-screen widgets: square, minimal square, compact, task strip, and wide. All show the native countdown and offer start, pause, resume, or continue without opening the app. They follow the Midnight theme with a dark palette, and Reduce Motion shows a still plant.

Plus adds four accent tints and independent controls for showing the mode and the task.

Why is my widget not updating?

First open Sproutime once so it can reschedule its updates. Then check Android's battery restrictions for Sproutime — aggressive battery saving can delay widget refreshes and alarms. Some launchers also need a moment to redraw after a restart.

If it still looks stuck, remove the widget and add it again from your home screen. Widgets are optional and the timer works without one.

Are notifications sent from a server?

No. Completion alerts are scheduled locally on your phone with Android's alarm APIs. There is no push notification service and no server that knows when your session ends. Firebase is used only for the optional tester-code programme, not for alerts.

Notifications are optional. If you decline the permission, the timer still runs and the session is still recorded.

Plans & billing

Optional upgrades, handled entirely by Google Play.

What is included in Free, Plus, and Grovekeeper?

Free includes timers, history and day logs, stats, CSV export and import, every theme, accessibility settings, and your garden.

Plus adds guided and custom routines, five offline soundscapes, weekly solo challenges, Year in Focus, widget tints and content controls, and garden layouts and seasons.

Grovekeeper is one payment for everything in Plus, kept forever, plus a leaf mark. It is not a subscription.

How much do the plans cost?

The planned prices are $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year for Plus, with a seven-day free trial on the subscription. Grovekeeper is planned at $49.99 as a one-time purchase.

Final prices, taxes, and availability are set by Google Play and shown in the app before you confirm. The website cannot take a payment.

The Plans screen says products are unavailable. Why?

Sproutime is still in pre-release and its products may not be published in Play Console yet. In that state the app deliberately refuses to start a purchase. Your free features and any existing local plan are unchanged.

Try again after the release is live. If the message says Google Play is unavailable, check your connection and Play Store sign-in, then use Check store connection.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Install Sproutime, sign in to Google Play with the same Google account you used to buy, open Settings → Plans, and choose Restore purchases. A connection is required. Restoring uses Google Play's record of the purchase; it does not upload your focus history.

How do I cancel Plus, and do I get a refund?

Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the paid period.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. Sproutime never receives your card details, so it cannot issue or process a refund directly. Use Google Play's order history to request one.

Is my payment information visible to Sproutime?

No. Google Play processes the transaction. The app receives only the purchase result and entitlement needed to unlock features. It never sees your card number, billing address, or Google account password.

Other questions

Is Sproutime suitable for children?

Sproutime is a general-audience productivity app, not a children's app. The core app collects no personal information and has no account or social features. A tester code can be redeemed without an email, but it creates an anonymous Firebase identifier. Parents can remove all local data by uninstalling the app. See the Children section of the privacy policy.

Where do the soundscapes come from?

The five recordings are real field recordings from Wikimedia Commons, bundled in the app: rain by cori (public domain), stream by stephan (public domain), forest by nille (public domain), fireplace by Glaneur de sons (CC BY 3.0), and night crickets by DrTrumpet (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Full credits are available inside the app under Soundscape → Sound credits. Playback is local and never streams.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email the support address in the contact card. Include your Android version, the Sproutime version, and what you expected to happen. Please do not send notes, tags, or screenshots you want to keep private — a description is enough.

Do not send payment details. Purchases and refunds must go through Google Play.
