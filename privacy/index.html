Privacy policy

Sproutime is built to keep your focus life private. The app has no account, no analytics, and no cloud sync. This page explains exactly what stays on your device, the one place the app talks to the internet, and the choices you have.

The short version

Sproutime is an offline-first Android app. Your timer settings, focus history, task labels, tags, notes, custom routines, challenge progress, garden, and Collection are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

There is no Sproutime account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no cloud backup. The app does not sell or share your focus data. Optional plans are bought through Google Play Billing; Google processes the payment and related purchase data under its own privacy policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Sproutime is published by [developer legal name], [business address]. For privacy questions, contact [contact email].

Because the app has no server and no account system, the developer does not receive the content you create in the app. The developer is responsible for the app itself, this website, and any purchase records Google Play makes available to developers.

2. What is stored on your device

All app data is kept in one private DataStore file named focus_grove inside the app's sandbox. Other apps cannot read it on a normal, non-rooted Android device. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for Sproutime, so this file is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone automatically.

Task labels, tags, and notes are optional. Please avoid entering sensitive information there. The app does not need it, and exported CSV files are not protected by the app once they leave private storage.

CSV export and import

If you choose Export history, Sproutime creates a CSV file and hands it to Android's system document picker, where you choose the destination. If you choose Import history, Android's picker lets you choose a file for the app to read. The app only reads the file you select. Once exported, the copy is outside Sproutime's private storage and is governed by where you saved it and any app that can access it.

3. What is never collected

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account.
  • No advertising identifier, device fingerprint, or contact list.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos, or health data.
  • No analytics events, session replays, crash reports, or telemetry.
  • No cloud sync, social feed, or public profile.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information, because there is none to sell or share.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, Firebase, Crashlytics, or social login.

4. Network connections and purchases

Sproutime requests the INTERNET permission solely so Google Play Billing can work. In the current build, the only network activity is the Play Billing library contacting Google Play when you open the Plans screen, start a purchase, or restore a previous purchase. The timer, history, garden, soundscapes, routines, and notes work without a connection and are not uploaded.

What Google Play receives

When you buy or restore Plus or Grovekeeper, Google Play processes the transaction. Google may receive your Google account, payment method, device and store information, and the product you selected. The app receives the purchase result and entitlement information it needs to unlock features. Sproutime does not receive or store your card number, billing address, or other payment credentials.

5. Permissions the app may request

Sproutime asks only for what a local timer needs. You can decline optional permissions and still use the core app.

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13+). Used for a local alert when a session ends. Declining means you may not see the alert, but the timer still runs.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM). Used, when Android permits it, to deliver a completion alert at the right moment. If unavailable, the app falls back to an inexact alarm.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED). Used only to restore notification and widget timing after your phone restarts. It does not start the app in the background for advertising or analytics.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE). Used only if you enable haptics.
  • Internet (INTERNET). Used only by Google Play Billing, as described above.

Notification permission, exact-alarm access, and battery settings are controlled by Android. You can change them at any time in the app's Settings screen or in Android Settings. Widgets are optional home-screen components; removing a widget removes its updates.

6. Sharing and third parties

Sproutime does not share app data with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. There is no server to share it with.

The only third-party service involved is Google Play Billing, for purchases. Google Play distributes the app and may collect information under its own policies when you download, install, review, or buy something. The developer may see aggregated sales, payout, and review information in Play Console, but not your payment credentials or your in-app focus history.

The developer may disclose information if legally required and if it is actually in their possession. Because focus content is never transmitted, the developer cannot disclose it.

This website links to Google Play and Google's policies. Following those links takes you to Google's services, which have their own privacy practices.

7. How long data is kept

App data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. There is no automatic expiry and no copy elsewhere.

  • Focus history, garden, Collection, routines, and settings: kept locally until you use Delete history or uninstall Sproutime.
  • Exported CSV files: kept wherever you saved them until you delete them. Sproutime cannot delete or recall an exported file.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google according to Google's policies and by the developer only as required for tax, accounting, and legal obligations.
  • Support correspondence: if you email support, the developer keeps the message only as long as needed to answer and meet legal obligations.

8. Deleting your data

Delete focus history in the app

  1. Open Sproutime and go to Settings.
  2. Find Delete history in the Data section.
  3. Confirm Delete history in the dialog.

This removes every saved session and everything derived from it: statistics, the garden, and Collection discoveries. It cannot be undone. Export a CSV first if you want a copy. Settings, routines, theme, and plan status are not cleared by this action.

Delete everything

Uninstalling Sproutime removes its private storage, including settings, routines, history, and plan status. Android may keep the app's data if you only clear the app's cache; to remove everything, use Clear storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Purchases

Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription or refund a purchase. Manage or cancel Plus in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Grovekeeper is a one-time purchase and can be restored with the same Google account after reinstalling.

9. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Use the app without creating an account or providing any identity information.
  • Keep or decline notification, exact-alarm, haptic, and widget permissions.
  • Export your history in a portable CSV format at any time.
  • Delete history or uninstall the app to remove local data.
  • Contact support to ask a privacy question or request help with data that the developer may hold (for example, a support email).

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data. Because Sproutime does not transmit your app data, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device using the tools above. For any request, contact [contact email]. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

The app does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, and it does not profile you for advertising.

10. Children

Sproutime is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The app does not ask for a name, email address, birth date, or any other personal information, and it does not transmit focus content. Because there is no account or server, the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact [contact email] and it will be deleted. Parents and guardians can also delete all local app data by uninstalling the app.

11. Security

Sproutime relies on Android's app sandbox to keep its DataStore file private. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for the app. The app contains no server, so there is no online account database to breach.

Your device's lock screen and encryption protect local storage. Keep your device updated and use a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure, and a rooted or compromised device can expose app data.

Exported CSV files are not encrypted by Sproutime. Store them somewhere you trust, and delete them when you no longer need them.

12. International transfers

Sproutime does not transfer your app data internationally because it does not transmit it. Google Play may process purchase and store data in countries other than yours under Google's own safeguards and policies. If you email support, your message travels through your email provider and may be processed where the developer or their email provider operates.

This website is plain HTML, CSS, and a small local script. It does not set cookies, use analytics, embed third-party fonts, or load tracking pixels. The fonts are self-hosted. Server logs from your hosting provider may record standard request information such as IP address, browser, and requested page for security and operation; that is controlled by the hosting provider.

The site links to Google Play and Google policies. Those external sites have their own privacy practices.
